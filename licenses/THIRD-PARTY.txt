Third-party material in FLOPOS
==============================

src/apps/lib/audio/gen/aactab.h
    The AAC spectral codebooks, generated by tools/build/mkaactab.py from the
    tables of the VisualOn AAC encoder (https://github.com/mstorsjo/vo-aacenc).
    Copyright 2003-2010, VisualOn, Inc.
    Licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0: see Apache-2.0.txt.
    Changed: only the tables were taken, and rewritten into one array.
    Its NOTICE file (https://github.com/mstorsjo/vo-aacenc/blob/master/NOTICE)
    says:
        Additional Codecs
        These files are Copyright 2003-2010 VisualOn, but released under
        the Apache2 License.

src/apps/lib/audio/gen/mp3tab.h
    The MPEG audio standard's tables, taken from PDMP3
    (https://github.com/technosaurus/PDMP3), which is in the public domain.

src/apps/lib/audio/gen/opustab.h
    The numbers RFC 6716 fixes for Opus's CELT layer (bands, bit allocation,
    energy probabilities, the pulse cache), generated by
    tools/build/mkopustab.py from libopus 1.4 (https://opus-codec.org/).
    Copyright 2001-2011 Xiph.Org and others.
    Licensed under the BSD 3-Clause License: see BSD-3-Clause-libopus.txt.
    Changed: only the tables were taken; the decoder (opus.c) is FLOPOS's own.

data/roots.dat
    The authorities secure websites are checked against, made by
    tools/build/mkroots.py from Mozilla's list of trusted root certificates
    (https://wiki.mozilla.org/CA), which Mozilla publishes under the
    Mozilla Public License 2.0 (https://mozilla.org/MPL/2.0/).

Everything else in FLOPOS is under the MIT License in LICENSE.
